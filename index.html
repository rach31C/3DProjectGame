Tutorial

Tutorial

  • Do you have what it takes to win?
  • There are 6 games you must play.

  • Controls
  • Use WASD to move around.
  • Use your mouse to look around.
  • Red Light, Green Light
  • You must reach the finish line while the doll's head is turned away. You must stop completely when she turns around.
  • Sugar Honeycombs (Dalgona)
  • Using a needle, you must perfectly carve out a specific shape (circle, triangle, star, or square) from a fragile honeycomb candy without breaking it. Click on the darken outline of the shape. Anywhere inside the shape...well you lose.
  • Tug of War
  • You will compete with a bot on a high platform; you must tug the rope to your platform. To tug, type on the correct number displayed on your screen. The losing team will die.
  • Marbles
  • In pairs, players play a game of their choice with 10 marbles each. The goal is to win all of your partner's marbles within the time limit.
  • Glass Stepping Stones
  • You must cross a bridge made of two types of glass: tempered (safe) and normal (breaks instantly). It is entirely a game of chance for those at the front of the line.
  • Squid Game
  • The final two players face off in "Red Light Green Light" arena, which they'll have to fight to the death. Last one alive wins!
  • Last, but not least, Good Luck!
